# Breakpoints
* None --- Extra small --------- <576px
* sm ----- Small --------------- ≥576px
* md ----- Medium -------------- ≥768px
* lg ----- Large --------------- ≥992px
* xl ----- Extra large --------- ≥1200px
* xxl ---- Extra extra large --- ≥1400px

# Containers
* .container --------- max-width at every breakpoint
* .container-_ ------- 100% width before _ breakpoint, max-width at and after _ breakpoint
* .container-fluid --- 100% width at every breakpoint
